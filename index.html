Chocoflan

A delicious dessert that combines chocolate cake and delicious flan

Ingredients

Cake:

  • 1/4 cup Smuckers candy topping (from a 12.25-oz./347-gr./12.25-oz)
  • 1 box Betty Crocker Super Moist Chocolate Fudge Cake Flour
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs

Custard:

  • 1 can (396 gr./ 14 oz) condensed milk (not evaporated)
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 4 eggs

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F/175°C. Spray a 12-cup springform pan with cooking spray. Pour and spread the caramel topping onto the bottom of the pan.
  2. In a large bowl, beat the cake mix, water, oil, and 3 eggs with an electric mixer on medium speed for 2 minutes, scraping the side of the bowl occasionally. Pour the batter into the pan, covering it with the caramel topping.
  3. Place the flan ingredients in the mixer. Cover and blend on high for 20 seconds or until smooth. Pour the mixture slowly and evenly over the batter. (The flan mix will blend into the batter, but they will separate during baking, forming one cake layer and one flan layer.) Spray a piece of aluminum foil (the size of the pan) with cooking spray and cover the pan with that side down, covering the pan and pressing it down tightly.
  4. Place the cake pan on a baking tray and add 2 cm / 1 inch of water to the tray.
  5. Bake for 1 hour and 20 minutes to ensure the cake and flan are fully baked (a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake should come out clean). Remove the pan from the water and place it on a cooling rack. Remove the foil. Let it cool at room temperature for 1 hour. Refrigerate it in the pan, uncovered, for 2 hours.
  6. Remove from the refrigerator. Run a thin metal spatula around the edges to loosen the cake. Place a serving platter upside down on top of the cake pan and turn it upside down. Remove the pan. Use a spoon to remove any caramel residue from the pan and pour it over the cake. Store loosely covered in the refrigerator.
